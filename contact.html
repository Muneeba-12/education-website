COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Educator courses, subscriptions, and certifications.

How do I receive my verified completion certificate?
Once you watch all required module lectures and submit the capstone portfolio project, our automated grading system and instructors review your submission. Upon passing, your verifiable digital certificate is instantly generated in your student portal with a unique credential ID and QR code.
Do I get lifetime access to purchased courses?
Yes! When you enroll in any individual course on Educator, you gain lifetime access to all lectures, downloadable code repositories, exercises, and all future curriculum updates at no additional cost.
What is the 30-day refund policy?
If you are not 100% satisfied with any course within 30 days of enrollment, you can request a full, no-questions-asked refund directly through your account dashboard.
Can I learn on mobile or tablet devices?
Absolutely. Educator is fully responsive and optimized for seamless viewing across smartphones, iPads, tablets, laptops, and large monitors.
